# Mojo Vision

> Mojo Vision is a Saratoga, California semiconductor and display company building a
> "wafers-in, wafers-out" micro-LED platform. Founded in 2015, it first developed the
> world's smallest, densest dynamic display for an AR smart contact lens, then pivoted
> in 2023 to commercializing micro-LED displays and optical interconnects. Its platform
> pairs a 300mm silicon architecture with GaN-on-Silicon emitters, proprietary quantum
> dots and micro-lens arrays, and has demonstrated a monolithic RGB panel at 4µm pixel
> pitch (6,350 ppi). Target markets are XR and AI-enabled glasses, automotive heads-up
> displays, large-format displays, and high-density optical I/O for AI infrastructure.
